Saving downloads on iPhone or iPad

Find your download

Choose Download image or Download text on the results page. Check Safari’s Downloads list or the Downloads folder in the Files app.

Save a screenshot instead

If the image download fails, take a screenshot of your results. Crop it to the ratings you want to share. You can also download a plain-text copy of the full list.
