About and privacy

What is MyList for?

MyList is a checklist for adults to think through their interests, relationship preferences, and boundaries before talking with a partner. Use your ratings to start a conversation and agree on consent together.

  • MyList lets you rate topics from favorites to hard limits or leave them blank. Use Question preferences to choose which topics you see. Use Customize to add an optional name, age, or role and pick a theme.
  • Results shows your ratings by category, lets you finish skipped questions, and gives you options to download a text file or image or copy a share link.
  • Compare uses a partner’s share link or JSON backup to show where your rated interests overlap, differ, or run into a hard limit.
  • Profiles lets you export a backup or import a backup or share link. You can fill blank fields in your active profile or import into a new named profile.

Where are my answers stored?

MyList saves your ratings, question preferences, optional profile details, theme, and age-gate choice in your browser’s storage for this site. They usually survive closing the tab or quitting the browser. To pick up where you left off, return to the same site address in the same browser profile, such as your personal Chrome profile.

Anyone who uses that browser profile can open your saved list. On a different device or in a different browser or profile, you’ll start with a blank list unless you import a copy. Clearing the site’s data in browser settings can erase your list. In private browsing, the browser typically discards it when the private session ends.

MyList runs comparisons and reads and creates files on your device. If you open Compare with a backup from Results, MyList briefly holds the file contents in that tab’s storage and removes them when Compare reads them.

Why save answers in the browser?

Sites that save answers to an online account keep a copy on their servers. With MyList, your ratings and profile stay out of an account database. You can fill out the checklist without giving an email address and decide who gets a copy. To move or recover your list and settings, keep a private JSON backup.

Hosting provider

Cloudflare hosts MyList. When your browser requests a page or other site file, Cloudflare may process your IP address and information about your browser and connection to deliver and protect the site. See Cloudflare’s Privacy Policy for details about how it handles visitor traffic.

What’s in a download or share link?

Text and image downloads can show your optional profile details, including your saved gender and partner preference. A JSON backup includes your saved ratings, even those hidden by your current filters, along with your settings and profile details in plain text.

A share link includes all saved ratings, even those hidden by your filters, and includes your results profile when you’ve entered one. This can include your name, age, role, gender, and partner preference. That information is encoded after the # in the URL. Your browser reads that part locally when opening the page. Anyone with the full link can view or forward your results.

How do I clear my data?

Open Clear MyList data, select the button, then confirm. This deletes your saved ratings, settings, profile details, and age-gate choice from this browser profile. To keep a copy, export a backup first.

Delete downloaded files separately. Share links contain their own copy of your results, so anyone who already has the link can still open it after you clear your browser data.
