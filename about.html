About & privacy

This adults-only quiz is for reflecting on your interests. Scores summarize how you rated the statements, using a weighted 1–5 agreement scale. The scoring rubric is unvalidated.

Your test data stays on your device

Your browser scores the test and stores your answers, age confirmation, and any profile details you enter. The test does not upload that data to Deviant Bound, Cloudflare, or another service. You can take the test without an online account.

Your age confirmation is stored for the current tab. This browser keeps your progress in Default and saved profiles between visits, along with any profile details and themes. Use Reset answers to clear the current test, or go to Profiles to reset progress or delete a saved profile.

Cloudflare and website traffic

Cloudflare can make the site load faster by caching public files closer to visitors and help keep it available during denial-of-service attacks. When your connection goes through Cloudflare, it can process information needed to deliver and protect the site, such as your IP address and details about the pages or files requested. See Cloudflare’s privacy policy.

Saving and sharing

Your browser creates backups, PNGs, and print or PDF reports locally. Backups contain your answers, optional profile details, and any imported results. PNGs and PDF reports include your scores and any profile details shown on the report. If you share a file or save it to a synced folder, your chosen app or storage provider may receive it.

A results link contains your role scores in the part of the URL after #. Your browser reads that part locally, and it is excluded from the request that loads the page. Anyone you give the link to can view the scores. Individual answers and profile details are excluded from the link.
